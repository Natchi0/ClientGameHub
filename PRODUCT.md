# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to find an online game and play with other people from the same community. They use the hub on desktop and mobile, before or between game sessions.

## Product Purpose

GameHub lets people create an account, find friends, chat, discover games, and join online matches from one place. A useful first visit makes the next game and the people available to play immediately legible.

## Positioning

An inferred product direction from the initial brief: social connection and game discovery coexist in the same hub, so choosing what to play should feel as direct as seeing who is ready to join.

## Operating Context

The core workflows are signing in or registering, choosing a game from a catalogue, finding friends, chatting, and joining online play.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Vue 3, TypeScript, Vite application.
- Authentication routes: sign in and registration.
- Current scope includes a basic dashboard with illustrative game and friend data; it does not implement live social or game services.
- The interface must work on mobile and desktop and support light and dark themes.

## Brand Commitments

The visual language should feel gamer-oriented without exaggeration: polished, usable, and not a generic AI/SaaS interface. It should avoid excessive gradients and card-heavy composition.

## Evidence on Hand

- Application name and initial copy: `GameHub`.
- Existing logo asset: `src/assets/logo.svg`.
- No supplied game art, testimonials, activity data, or product metrics. Dashboard content is explicitly illustrative.

## Product Principles

1. Put the next social or play action within immediate reach.
2. Make game information easy to scan instead of decorative.
3. Let the interface carry a quiet game-culture energy without overwhelming gameplay choices.
4. Treat responsive and theme support as first-class product behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, and layouts that reflow without losing actions or information.
